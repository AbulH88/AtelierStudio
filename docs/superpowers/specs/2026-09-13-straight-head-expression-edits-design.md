# Straight Head and Expression Edits

## Goal

Create eight non-destructive edits of the supplied character portraits. Each edit straightens the subject's head to a forward-facing, level alignment and adds a distinct, natural facial expression.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs are the eight PNG files in `E:\DataSet\MyNew`. Each output preserves its corresponding image's environment, wardrobe, pose, lighting, and character identity. Edited files will be saved alongside the originals with ` - straight expression` appended to the filename.

## Edit treatment

- Preserve facial identity, hair, skin texture, clothing, accessories, body pose, and room.
- Rotate and subtly recompose only the head so it appears level and facing camera naturally.
- Keep realism: pores, freckles, stray hairs, camera texture, and existing lighting.
- Do not introduce text, logos, or watermarks.

## Expression assignments

1. Moody Butterfly Portrait in a Hallway — soft smile
2. Post-Workout Hallway Selfie — amused smile
3. Soft-Lit Bedroom Portrait — thoughtful expression
4. Sultry Kitchen Counter Portrait — confident expression
5. Warm Apartment Kitchen Portrait — relaxed warm smile
6. Gothic Bedroom Portrait with Moth Choker — composed, slightly serious expression
7. Gothic Vanity Portrait in Warm Light — playful small smile
8. Kitchen Confidence Portrait — calm neutral expression

## Validation

Inspect every output for straight facial alignment, a readable but subtle assigned expression, preserved identity, and no unexpected changes outside the face/head adjustment.
